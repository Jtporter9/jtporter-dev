## The problem

After acquiring LowestMed, RetailMeNot rebranded the product as **Rx Saver**. The frontend was still the original Angular code, which was hard to test and slowed the team down.

## What I did

- Helped migrate the full frontend from Angular to **React and Redux**, alongside a redesign.
- Pushed for testing as we went, raising frontend unit-test coverage by **40%** with Jest and Enzyme.
- Reviewed peer code for function, design, and consistency.
- Helped with the backend move from .NET to Java/Spring.
- Led the redesign and refactor of the marketing site's WordPress theme.

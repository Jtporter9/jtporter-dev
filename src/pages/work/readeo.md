## The product

Readeo lets kids and their loved ones read picture books together over video chat, which is perfect for grandparents and parents who travel. It runs on readeo.com, the web app, and native iOS and Android apps.

## What I did

I was hired as the engineering lead. I led **seven engineers**: organizing work, reviewing code, and unblocking the team. I also ran sprints and task management, much like a product manager, and acted as head of engineering across the company's sites and apps.

The big milestone was **launching the Readeo app** on the App Store and Google Play.
